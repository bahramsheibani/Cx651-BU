(1) Compared to plot-single.png, the concurrent runs take very much longer and show more variation in execution time. CPU usage is also lower and varies around 25-40%, rather than increasing with matrix size, because the ten processes are sharing the available CPU resources.

(2) Compared to plot-multi.png, plot-taskset.png shows execution time increasing much more sharply as the matrix size and

Since all processes are restricted to the same CPU, they  take turns and experience greater contention
