(1) Compared to plot-single.png, the concurrent runs take substantially longer and show more variation in execution time. CPU usage is also lower and fluctuates around 25-40%, rather than increasing with matrix size, because the ten processes are sharing the available CPU resources.

(2) Compared to plot-multi.png, plot-taskset.png shows execution time increasing much more sharply as the matrix size and number of competing jobs increase.
